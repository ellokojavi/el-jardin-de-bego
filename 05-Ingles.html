Unit 3 · What we eat? — Food vocabulary and healthy habits

Bego · 5° básico · Repaso 2026

Objetivos de Aprendizaje (Bases Curriculares, Mineduc — Unidad 3)

  • Comprender información básica sobre alimentación, oral y escrita.
  • Expresar gustos y preferencias sobre comidas.
  • Usar there is / there are, some / any y contables e incontables.
  • Describir hábitos con adverbios de frecuencia.

Estimated time: 3 horas, repartidas en 2 sesiones.

Las instrucciones van en inglés, con apoyo en español cuando hace falta.

Group Words

Watch out / Ojo

  • strawberry → strawberries (la y se cambia por -ies).
  • fish es igual en singular y plural.

Section 2 · Likes and dislikes

How to say it

Structure Example Español
I like + food I like apples. Me gustan las manzanas.
I don’t like + food I don’t like onions. No me gustan las cebollas.
I love + food I love strawberries. Me encantan las frutillas.
I hate + food I hate soft drinks. Odio las bebidas.
My favourite food is… My favourite food is fish. Mi comida favorita es el pescado.

Asking and answering

Do you like carrots?
Yes, I do. / No, I don’t.

What’s your favourite fruit?
My favourite fruit is watermelon.

Con he y she se agrega -s al verbo:

  • She likes bananas. · He doesn’t like cheese.

  1. Complete with like or likes: a) I ______ pasta. b) My sister ______ milk. c) They ______ fruit.
  2. Write the negative form: a) I like onions. → b) He likes soft drinks. →
  3. Answer about yourself in full sentences: a) Do you like fish? b) What’s your favourite vegetable? c) What food do you hate?
  4. Translate into English: a) Me encanta el pan. b) A mi mamá no le gusta el café. c) ¿Te gustan las arvejas?
  5. Write three sentences about your family: one with likes, one with doesn’t like, one with loves.

Section 3 · There is / There are · Countable and uncountable

Countable and uncountable nouns

Countable (se pueden contar, tienen plural): an apple, two eggs, three carrots.

Uncountable (no se cuentan uno por uno, no tienen plural): milk, water, rice, bread, cheese, butter, meat.

Para contar los incontables se usa un envase o medida: a glass of milk, a slice of bread, a piece of cheese, a bottle of water, a cup of tea.

There is / There are

Form Use Example
There is Singular y todos los incontables There is an orange. · There is some milk.
There are Plural contable There are five bananas.
There isn’t / There aren’t Negativo There isn’t any bread. · There aren’t any eggs.
Is there…? / Are there…? Pregunta Is there any juice? · Are there any tomatoes?

Some and any

  • some → en oraciones afirmativas: There is some rice.
  • any → en negativas y preguntas: There isn’t any rice. Is there any rice?

How much / How many

  • How many + contable: How many apples are there?
  • How much + incontable: How much milk is there?

✏️ Now you try

  1. Classify as countable (C) or uncountable (U): water, banana, rice, egg, cheese, carrot, bread, tomato.
  2. Complete with is or are: a) There ______ two lemons. b) There ______ some butter. c) There ______ five eggs. d) There ______ any water?
  3. Complete with some or any: a) There is ______ cheese in the fridge. b) There aren’t ______ potatoes. c) Is there ______ juice?
  4. Complete with How much or How many: a) ______ oranges are there? b) ______ milk is there?
  5. Look at your own kitchen and write four true sentences using there is and there are.

Section 4 · Healthy habits

Frequency adverbs

English Español Frecuencia
always siempre 100%
usually normalmente 80%
often a menudo 60%
sometimes a veces 40%
hardly ever casi nunca 10%
never nunca 0%

Se colocan antes del verbo principal: I always eat breakfast. · She never drinks soft drinks.

Should and shouldn’t

Sirven para dar consejos.

  • You should drink eight glasses of water a day.
  • You should do sixty minutes of exercise every day.
  • You shouldn’t eat junk food every day.
  • You shouldn’t smoke. It’s very bad for your lungs and your heart.

Useful sentences about healthy habits

I eat fruit and vegetables every day.
I drink water instead of soft drinks.
I sleep nine hours every night.
I wash my hands before meals.
I ride my bike after school.

✏️ Now you try

  1. Order from most to least frequent: sometimes · never · always · often.
  2. Put the adverb in the right place: a) I eat breakfast. (always) b) She drinks soft drinks. (never) c) We play football. (sometimes)
  3. Complete with should or shouldn’t: a) You ______ eat vegetables every day. b) You ______ smoke. c) You ______ sleep nine hours. d) You ______ drink three soft drinks a day.
  4. Write four sentences about your own habits using four different frequency adverbs.
  5. Give three pieces of advice to a friend who wants to be healthier, using should and shouldn’t.
